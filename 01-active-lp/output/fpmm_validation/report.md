Same 44 designed histories; normalized lambda .15, explicit matched founder funding. Results are distributional diagnostics, not an equilibrium or causal information-timing experiment.

The comparison fixes requested share quantities, not equal expenditure or equal realized prices. FPMM returned outcome tokens are included in LP wealth. The scheduled deposits use the LMSR reference price at entry for both mechanisms. Both hold capital to settlement. A common initial funding amount does not equalize liquidity functions, risk, or opportunity cost.

Each funding directory contains complete requested bundles and per-mechanism result rows. summary.json separates designed families and records any failed checks.
